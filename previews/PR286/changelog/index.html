Changes when updating to v0.2 from v0.1.x

Changed

  • The suffix WithGravity is removed from the names of the compressible Euler equations, since all of them include gravity: the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity{1,2,3}D, the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity{2,3}D, the CompressibleEulerInternalEnergyEquationsWithGravity2D, and the CompressibleEulerVectorInvariantEquationsWithGravity3D are renamed to the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquations{1,2,3}D, the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquations{2,3}D, the CompressibleEulerInternalEnergyEquations2D, and the CompressibleEulerVectorInvariantEquations3D (#279).
  • The CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity3D, the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity3D, and the CompressibleEulerVectorInvariantEquationsWithGravity3D store the geopotential as an auxiliary variable of Trixi.jl (see Trixi.nauxvariables) instead of as the last variable of the state. Thus, they have one variable fewer, their pointwise functions, including user-defined source terms and boundary conditions, take the auxiliary variables as additional arguments after the states, e.g., flux_ec(u_ll, u_rr, aux_ll, aux_rr, normal_direction, equations), source_terms_coriolis(u, aux, x, t, equations), and cons2prim(u, aux, equations) (also cons2entropy and entropy, as required by Trixi.jl v0.18), and SemidiscretizationHyperbolic requires the keyword argument aux_field, e.g., aux_field_gravity_earth or aux_field_gravity_small_earth. With adaptive time stepping, results change slightly since the geopotential no longer enters the error estimate of the time integrator (#267).
  • The CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity2D, the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity2D, and the CompressibleEulerInternalEnergyEquationsWithGravity2D store the geopotential as an auxiliary variable of Trixi.jl (see Trixi.nauxvariables) instead of as the last variable of the state. Thus, they have one variable fewer, their pointwise functions, including user-defined source terms and boundary conditions, take the auxiliary variables as additional arguments after the states, e.g., flux_ec(u_ll, u_rr, aux_ll, aux_rr, normal_direction, equations) and cons2prim(u, aux, equations) (also cons2entropy and entropy, as required by Trixi.jl v0.18), and SemidiscretizationHyperbolic requires the keyword argument aux_field, e.g., the new aux_field_gravity. Since auxiliary variables are only available for P4estMeshes, the elixirs elixir_potential_temperature_inertia_gravity_waves.jl and elixir_energy_inertia_gravity_waves.jl use a P4estMesh instead of a StructuredMesh. With adaptive time stepping, results change slightly since the geopotential no longer enters the error estimate of the time integrator (#268).

Removed

  • The CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquations1D, the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquations2D, and the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquations3D without gravity are removed. Use the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity1D, the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity2D, and the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity3D instead, which include gravity as a nonconservative term and are now called CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquations{1,2,3}D. With a zero geopotential, they reduce to the equations without gravity, as in elixir_potential_temperature_ec.jl and elixir_potential_temperature_taylor_green_vortex.jl, and gravity can be added as a source term, as in elixir_potential_temperature_robert_bubble.jl (#276).
  • TrixiAtmo.jl now consistently uses P4estMesh for the Cartesian equations in 2D and 3D. Thus, the methods based on orientation (for TreeMesh and StructuredMesh) of max_abs_speed_naive, max_abs_speed, flux_ec_rain, boundary_condition_slip_wall, and boundary_condition_simple_slip_wall are removed for the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquations2D, the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquations3D, the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity2D, the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity3D, the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity2D, the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity3D, the CompressibleMoistEulerEquations2D, and the CompressibleRainyEulerEquations2D. The methods of boundary_condition_slip_wall with the additional argument direction, which fixed the sign convention of StructuredMesh, are removed for the CompressibleMoistEulerEquations2D and the CompressibleRainyEulerEquations2D. Use the methods based on normal_direction together with P4estMesh instead. The physical flux flux(u, orientation, equations) is kept since VolumeIntegralWeakForm uses it also on P4estMesh. The 1D equations and the equations in covariant form are not affected (#263).

Changes in the v0.1 lifecycle

Added

  • The CompressibleEulerMoistPotentialTemperatureEquations3D are the compressible Euler equations with gravity with the virtual potential temperature as prognostic variable and the moisture variables of a microphysics scheme, whose right-hand sides are given by source_terms_microphysics. The KesslerMicrophysics is the warm-rain Kessler microphysics of DCMIP2016 with water vapor, cloud water, and falling rain water. The equations store the geopotential and the upward unit vector, which gives the direction of the falling water, as auxiliary variables of Trixi.jl, e.g., given by aux_field_gravity_earth. Their volume flux flux_kennedy_gruber_souza_etal supports FluxTurbo with the KesslerMicrophysics. The moist baroclinic wave of DCMIP2016 is available as initial_condition_moist_baroclinic_instability (#271).
  • JacobianVerticalTwoSided enables horizontally explicit, vertically implicit (HEVI) time integration of the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity3D on a P4estMeshCubedSphereTopography as linear operator of linearly implicit methods, i.e., Rosenbrock-W methods such as SSPRW32 and linear IMEX methods such as ARS343, also on GPUs (#243).
  • The CompressibleEulerVectorInvariantEquationsWithGravity3D are the compressible Euler equations with gravity in vector-invariant form with the potential temperature as prognostic variable. They come with the entropy and total energy conservative volume flux flux_etec, which supports FluxTurbo, and the entropy-stable and well-balanced surface flux flux_lmars_es (#253).
  • The new nonconservative flux flux_nonconservative_isothermal is a well-balanced gravity term for isothermal background states of the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity2D and the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity3D (#248).
  • GPU support including adaptation to Float32 via trixi_adapt (#242).

Changed

  • Fixes a bug in flux_nonconservative_waruszewski_etal for the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity2D and the CompressibleEulerEnergyEquationsWithGravity3D: The gravity term of the momentum did not use the density of Eq. (73d) of Waruszewski et al. (2022). Thus, results of simulations with flux_nonconservative_waruszewski_etal change except for isothermal states. The previous gravity term is available as flux_nonconservative_isothermal (#248).
  • The gravity term flux_nonconservative_waruszewski_etal of the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity1D, the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity2D, and the CompressibleEulerPotentialTemperatureEquationsWithGravity3D is renamed to flux_nonconservative_isothermal, since it does not correspond to the one of Waruszewski et al. (2022) (#248).
